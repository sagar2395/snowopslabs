# SnowOps Labs Trademark Policy (DRAFT)

> **Status: DRAFT — pending legal review and trademark registration** (manual
> actions B3/B4). This policy takes effect once the wordmark is registered. Until
> then it states intent.

"**SnowOps Labs**", the SnowOps Labs logo, and related marks (the "Marks") are
trademarks of the project owner. The Marks are **not** licensed under the
[Apache-2.0 license](LICENSE) — Apache-2.0 covers the *code*, not the *brand*
(see License §6).

This policy exists so the community can use and build on SnowOps Labs freely while
preventing confusion about what is "official."

## You may, without asking

- Use the Marks to **truthfully refer** to the SnowOps Labs project (e.g. "built
  with SnowOps Labs", "a SnowOps Labs scenario pack").
- Use the word "SnowOps Labs" in blog posts, talks, tutorials, and community
  content that describes or discusses the project.
- Redistribute the unmodified software under Apache-2.0, keeping the Marks and
  NOTICE intact.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use the Marks (or a confusingly similar name/logo) as the name of **your**
  product, service, company, or a **fork**, in a way that implies official status
  or endorsement.
- Use the Marks for a **commercial offering** (hosted service, paid distribution,
  "SnowOps Labs Cloud", certification using the name) without a trademark license.
- Modify the logo or create derivative logos.

If you distribute a **modified** version, you must rename it so users aren't
misled into thinking it's official SnowOps Labs.

## Forks

Forking the Apache-2.0 code is welcome. A fork must use a **different name** and
must not use the SnowOps Labs Marks except to note its origin (e.g. "a fork of
SnowOps Labs").

## Questions / permission requests

Contact **admin@snowops.net**
